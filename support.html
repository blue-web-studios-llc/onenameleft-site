How do I play?

You get the whole list of people who were present and a chain of clues. Each clue describes something true of the culprit. Cross out everyone a clue does not describe, one clue at a time, until one name is left — then make your accusation.

I bought cases but they’re gone, or I got a new phone.

Open the case store and tap “Restore a purchase”. Your purchases are tied to your App Store or Google Play account and will come back.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your device. It stays there unless you delete the app or clear its data. Progress does not sync between devices.

I was charged but didn’t get my content, or I want a refund.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple or Google. If content you paid for isn’t unlocking, tap “Restore a purchase” first, then email us and we’ll help. For refunds, contact the App Store or Google Play directly.

Do you collect my data?

No. The game has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and nothing you do in it leaves your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Is any of this about real people?

No — every case and every name is invented. Any resemblance to a real person is coincidental.
